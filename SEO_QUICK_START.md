## 🚀 To Get Your First Rankings (This Week)

### Step 1: Publish Your First Post (or Refresh One)
- Ensure clinic has these fields filled:
  - `authorQualification` (e.g., "BDS, MDS Prosthodontics")
  - `authorPhotoUrl` (Doctor's headshot)
  - `authorBio` (2-3 sentence bio)

### Step 2: Verify Post Quality
```
Checklist for each post:
☐ Title includes main keyword + city (70 chars max)
☐ First paragraph mentions keyword in first sentence
☐ Minimum 1,500 words (2,000+ ideal)
☐ At least 3 H2 subheadings with keyword variation
☐ 3-4 FAQ sections (### Question format)
☐ Image alt text includes keyword
☐ CTA button to booking page
```

### Step 3: Submit to Google Search Console
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "URL inspection"
3. Paste your post URL
4. Click "Request indexing"

**Expected:** Indexed within 24-48 hours (breadcrumbs speed this up)

### Step 4: Monitor Position
After 1 week, in Google Search Console:
1. Go to "Performance" tab
2. Filter by your post
3. Expected initial position: **15-50**
